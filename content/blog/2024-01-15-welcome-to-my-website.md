# Welcome to My Website

I'm excited to launch my new personal website! This site will serve as a hub for sharing my research, thoughts, and updates about my work in machine learning and artificial intelligence.

## What You'll Find Here

This website includes:

- **Research**: Information about my current and past research projects
- **Publications**: Links to my academic papers and preprints
- **Blog**: Regular posts about ML research, academic life, and industry insights

## Built with Simplicity in Mind

This site is built using a custom static site generator written in Python. The design prioritizes:

- Fast loading times
- Clean, readable content
- Mobile-friendly responsive design
- Accessibility

Thanks for visiting, and I hope you find the content useful!
